---
title: Dual-Glo luciferase assay (lab version)
short: Dual-Glo
duration: 45 min
tags: [luciferase, reporter assay, 24-well, 96-well]
vessel: 24-well
materials:
  - Dual-Glo Luciferase Reagent (buffer + substrate combined, stored at −80 °C, thaw at RT)
  - Dual-Glo Stop & Glo Reagent (substrate 1:100 in buffer, prepare fresh, do not store)
  - 96-well plate compatible with the luminometer
---

## 1. Prepare reagents
Thaw Dual-Glo Luciferase Reagent at room temperature.
Dilute Stop & Glo Substrate 1:100 into Stop & Glo Buffer.
warning: Prepare Stop & Glo immediately before use. Do not store.

## 2. Reduce medium
Reduce the medium in each 24-well to 100 µL, for a 1:1 ratio with the reagent.

## 3. Add luciferase reagent
per_well: 100 µL Dual-Glo Luciferase Reagent
Mix gently.

## 4. Lyse
Cover the plate and incubate on the shaker at room temperature. Protect from light.
timer: 15m

## 5. Transfer
Transfer 100 µL from each 24-well into a 96-well plate.
note: Pop bubbles with a needle if needed.

## 6. Read firefly
Measure firefly luminescence.

## 7. Add Stop & Glo
per_well: 100 µL Stop & Glo Reagent
Mix thoroughly.

## 8. Second incubation
Cover and shake at room temperature. Protect from light.
timer: 10-15m

## 9. Read Renilla
Measure Renilla luminescence in the same well order as the firefly reading.

## 10. Analyse
Firefly / Renilla ratio per well, normalised to the control wells.
